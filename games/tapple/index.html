How it works

Speak · tap · pass

  1. One player is up. Tap Start turn when they are ready.
  2. They say a fitting answer aloud. The host taps its unused starting letter.
  3. After the needed letters are marked, tap Pass turn.
  4. If the group rejects an answer or time runs out, mark that player out. The last player earns the card.

The group decides what counts. This board never judges answers automatically.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space
Start, pause, or resume a turn
A–Z
Mark an available letter during a running turn
Enter
Pass after required letters are marked
P
Pause or resume for a group decision
